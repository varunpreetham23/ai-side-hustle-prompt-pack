# Client Acquisition Prompts

## 1. Ideal Client Research
Act as a market research expert. I offer [your service, e.g. AI-assisted content writing / LinkedIn ghostwriting / automation setup] for [target audience, e.g. small business owners / coaches / SaaS founders].

Help me define my ideal client profile in detail:
- Demographics and firmographics
- Main pain points related to my service
- Where they hang out online
- What language they use when describing their problems
- Buying triggers (what makes them ready to pay)

Make it specific and actionable.
