# AI Side Hustle Prompt Pack

A practical collection of high-quality AI prompts designed for freelancers and people with limited time who want to make money using AI tools like Grok, Claude, or ChatGPT.

These prompts are focused on real outcomes: finding clients, writing proposals, creating content, building digital products, and packaging services — not generic fluff.

## What's Included

- **Client Acquisition** – Research, outreach, and proposal prompts
- **Content Creation** – Social posts, LinkedIn, email sequences, and YouTube scripts
- **Digital Products** – Ideas, structure, and sales copy for templates & prompt packs
- **Service Packaging** – Pricing, offers, and positioning
- **Research & Lead Generation** – Fast ways to find and qualify opportunities

## How to Use

1. Copy any prompt
2. Replace the `[brackets]` with your specific details
3. Paste into Grok, Claude, ChatGPT, or any strong AI
4. Edit the output to match your voice and add real examples

**Tip:** Always give the AI context about your niche, experience level, and target client for better results.
